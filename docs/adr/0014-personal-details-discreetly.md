# 0014. Personal details go in quietly

Lars, 2026-11-14, answering the plan page's "You, in the game" questions.

## Decision
- The school banner in the parade at Slottet is Høyenhall skole.
- Home, where Nabolaget ends, is Kværnertoppen in Gamle Oslo. Gamle Oslo is the personal part of
  the city; the front page is already a walk through it.
- Kruttverket, the old gunpowder works in Kværnerdalen, stands in Nabolaget shortly before home
  and gets careful detail. In an upstairs window hang a few paper cranes on threads.
- Details of this kind are put in without explanation. Nothing in the game's words, the code's
  comments, the tests, the commit messages or these documents says why a detail is there. If you
  know, you know. Ask Lars before adding, moving or explaining one.
- The repository is public. No street addresses in it. Neighbourhood and place names are fine.

## Consequences
- A later session will meet details that look arbitrary. They are not. Do not tidy them away.
- Personal facts Lars has told a session belong in that session's private memory, not here.
