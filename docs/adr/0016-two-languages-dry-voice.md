# 0016. Norwegian and English, in a dry voice

From the front page, kept as a rule in `PLAN.md` §3 and every order.

## Decision
- Every word the games show exists in Norwegian (for Norwegian browsers) and English (for
  everyone else). Kit words are in `packages/pixelkit/src/i18n.ts`; each app and level adds its own.
- Place names stay Norwegian in both.
- The voice is dry and short: a sign states a fact and lets it be funny. ("Never taken by siege.
  You are welcome to try." "Runesmia and Verkstedet moved down to the quay. The rent was lower.")
  No exclamation marks except the one the front page already had, no tutorial chatter, no puns.
- Two to four signs per level.
- The ending card shows the visitor's language with the other beneath it.

## Not chosen
- English only. The site is a Norwegian's, about Oslo.
- A narrator or dialogue. Signs and bubbles only.
