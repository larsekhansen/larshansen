# 0010. Benches, an earned dash, two bosses

Lars, 2026-11-14: "Add a splash of Hollow Knight maybe, or other famous platformers." Which
splashes is the planner's choice.

## Decision
- **Benches.** A park bench is a checkpoint he chooses to use (up to sit): hearts refill and the
  spot is saved. Flags save on touch and do not heal.
- **An ability from the line.** Clearing Holmenkollen gives the ski pole: a dash. Levels 6 to 10
  may require it after teaching it; levels 1 to 5 never do, but their secrets may use it. In
  levels 6 to 10 he always has it, because those stations only open after Holmenkollen.
- **Two bosses,** each three hits, a full-second telegraph before every attack, a flag and a
  bench right before: the troll of Nordmarka (turned to stone by dawn light and his headlamp)
  and the snow plough in Nabolaget (one bounce, one dash, one ride).
- **Juice:** a two-step pause and a 2 px nudge on a stomp or a hit, dust, coins that chime up a scale.
- Mechanics are borrowed; no character, name, art, level or tune from any existing game.

## Consequences
- Earlier levels gain something on a return visit (secrets that want the pole or the feather).
- The stations' unlock order guarantees the pole where it is required.

## Not chosen
- A pogo or a weapon. The stomp is the only attack.
- A map to buy, charms, a currency shop.
- Wall jump or cling.
- A boss per level. Two keep them events.
- Making the feather a line ability. It stays the maze's reward (0007).
