# Adversarial review of line 2, 15 November 2026

Reviewer: Claude (Opus 5.5), asked to find what is broken, unfair, confusing, ugly or incoherent in
line 2 of Vikingfjorden (`apps/viking`): Bygdøy, Vigelandsparken, Korketrekkeren, Østensjøvannet,
Bjørvika, Gamlebyen, the shield, the sledge, the sea serpent and the end of the line. Nothing under
`apps/viking/src`, `packages/`, `infra/` or `.github/` was changed. Checkout: `main` at `3ec3fc0` (the
merge of PR #61), level with `origin/main`. New review scripts are in `apps/viking/tools/review/`
(untracked; listed at the end).

Everything below was measured with the real rules (`game/play.ts` and the levels' own `rules`) on this Pi,
read from the code with line numbers, or seen in headless Firefox screenshots of the built page. Where
something is only reasoned, it says **by reading**. "Column" is a 16-pixel map column; ±k is a timing
error of up to k physics steps on every key edge (±2 a careful adult, ±4 a casual player or a child, ±8
someone fighting the keyboard), as in the first review.

**About the builders' numbers.** `bun sloppy.ts` reproduces `line2/summary.md` exactly: it always plays
seeds 1 to 30, the seeds the levels were tuned against. Everything in section A here is on fresh seeds
(101 to 200, a hundred runs per level and k; `fresh.ts`), which nobody had looked at.

---

## 1. Verdict

**A careful player (±2)** clears every line 2 level at the first attempt: 100 of 100 runs on each of the
six, no Game over, 0.2 to 1.3 hearts a level and about a quarter of a life over the whole line. **A casual
player (±4)** also gets through: 97 to 100 of 100 per level, at most one Game over in a hundred, 1.0 to 1.7
hearts a level for Bygdøy to Bjørvika and 3.6 for Gamlebyen, about one and a half lives over the six. The
**hesitating player** (stops 0.5 to 1.5 s before one move in five) loses at most three quarters of a heart
more, except on the lake (2.8 hearts against 1.4, the swans, as intended). Only **a clumsy player (±8)** falls off a cliff, and not where one
would expect: Bjørvika ends in Game over 63 times in 100, Bygdøy 46, Gamlebyen 13.

Nothing in line 2 traps him. No dead end in any level, none in 48 mixed states of Gamlebyen's rising
stones, no way to stop the sledge or to be put back without it, no statue that can seal a way, and every
flag and bench resumes. That is better than line 1 was at its review. What is wrong is quieter: line 2
does not get harder than line 1 until its last level, its finale is hard for the wrong reasons, and much of
what line 2 tells or gives the player is never seen.

**The three worst things**

1. **The serpent's quay has an invisible edge, and the fight can stall there for ever with a hint that
   is wrong.** The fight is cols 246 to 271, but the quay's planks run from col 236. Left of col 245 (the
   serpent's own bench is at 243, and a knock-back lands a player there) the ball vanishes in mid-air over
   the planks and the tail stops short; nothing can hit him and nothing happens, while every ten seconds
   it says "Hold the shield up as it spits" — also to a player who is holding it up (D1). And there is no
   flag at the fight: a lost life without having sat on the bench sends him back across the harbour's two
   longships, while Game over puts him at the bench (B1).
2. **Line 2 is not a step up from line 1, and the finale is hard for the wrong reasons.** At ±4 levels 11
   to 15 lose 1.0 to 1.7 hearts a run, less than line 1's second half (Akerselva 2.5, Nordmarka 2.3,
   Ekeberg 3.3). Gamlebyen is the hardest level in the game (3.6), but its hearts come from six
   maximum-width jumps over water (the last one 0.50 hearts a try at ±4, over the order's 0.4 cap, which
   the builders' tool never measured), an owl over the first pit, and the serpent's tail. The serpent itself,
   once understood, is beaten in about 30 s with 1.4 hearts and no Game over in 60 sloppy fights (A1, A2,
   I1, D5).
3. **What line 2 teaches and gives is mostly unseen.** The help line hides at his first move and never
   comes back, so "Hold down for the shield" and the sledge's "down to duck" are effectively never shown
   (C3). The map's banner says "T-banen, Line 1" on every arrival at a station, line 2's too (F1). The
   reward for all sixteen runes, a longship on the map, sits under the help line and is on screen about 25
   s in every two minutes (F2). The serpent is a 34-pixel dark-teal neck that is up for under a third of
   its own fight, its dazed head reads as a crate, and the dawn that ends the line is a flat peach disc
   beside the moon that the card covers 1.4 s later (G1).

**The ten things to fix, most important first**

1. Make the whole quay the fight, or end the quay where the fight ends (water at cols 236 to 245, or
   `x0 = 236`), and do not say "hold the shield up" when the ball never reached him (D1).
2. A flag at the serpent's quay (col 244), as the troll and the plough have one beside their bench (B1).
3. Show the help line again for a few seconds whenever its text changes (the shield given, the sledge),
   and on a level's first frame (C3).
4. Make line 2 rise: Bjørvika and Vigelandsparken up a step, Bygdøy as the warm-up it is; take
   Gamlebyen's difficulty out of max-width gaps (3-tile stones or 2.5-tile gaps) and put it into the
   serpent (I1, A1).
5. Give the serpent presence: taller (60 px), lighter rim, a head that looks like a head when dazed, a tail
   with a fin; and hold the card back until the dawn has come (3 to 4 s) (G1).
6. The map's banner: give `kartDef` the landmark "Lines 1 and 2" once line 2 is open (F1).
7. The longship: draw it just under the deck, above the help line, and let it stand still in front of the
   last station when he arrives there (F2).
8. Move Gamlebyen's owl off the dig, and make the jump-landing test look at a flyer's whole rise (A3).
9. A hint for the serpent's tail, and its warning made visible (a fin and spray at the quay's end) (D3).
10. A size check for the first script in the build or the deploy: 0.82 kB are left and nothing enforces the
    60 kB (J1).

Smaller: Nordmarka's troll is frozen for ever by a shield held at the arena's entrance, with no hint (C1);
"Hopp på hodet" reads as "dive in head first" beside the water (H1); "På kjelken er pil ned å dukke" (H2);
the sloppy player's "mistakes" profile is broken by its walks (A5).

**A1. major — Gamlebyen's stepping stones are six maximum-width jumps over water, one over the order's
cap, and the builders' tool never measured them.** `levels/gamlebyen.ts:66`: two-tile stones with
three-tile gaps (48 px of a 54 px jump) at cols 103–105, 108–110, 113–115, 118–120, then two tiles. The
last full gap costs **0.50 hearts a try at ±4** (38 % of tries fall), the others 0.20 to 0.28; at ±8 the
stones are the level's top killer (1.16 hearts a run, 43 lives in 100 runs). `line2/summary.md` says the
stones cost "about 0.07 a try": `obstacles.ts` only tries moves named jump or leap
(`obstacles.ts:12`), and these are named "onto the stepping stone at …", so the 0.4 rule of order 16 was
never checked for them. Commit `c6cfade` moved them further apart to raise the level's hearts. The first
review named this exact shape (max-width gaps over water) as the cheapest kind of difficulty. **Fix:**
three-tile stones or 2.5-tile gaps, and get the finale's difficulty from the serpent (I1); make
`allmoves.ts` the per-jump measure.

**A2. minor — the shield stretches cost 0.4 to 0.5 a try for a player who raises the shield at the last
moment.** Østensjøvannet's diving gulls (0.50) and Bygdøy's passage (0.42) at ±4: the route raises the
shield just before each throw, and ±4 steps on top of six steps to raise it is often late. A person who
raises it when the wind-up starts and holds it loses nothing, which is ADR 0024's point; the sloppy model
overstates these. Worth knowing before tuning on them.

**A3. minor — an owl over the first pit of the finale.** `levels/gamlebyen.ts:212`: a flyer at col 27,
row 15, range 2, over the dig at cols 24–25, and the route lands the leap over it at col 27. It is the
level's first enemy and its third biggest cost: 0.42 hearts a run at ±4 (0.49 hesitating), 0.48 at ±8.
The test that keeps patrols away from jumps over pits (`levels/levels.test.ts:331–341`) only looks at
enemies whose base row is within two tiles of the take-off or landing, so a flyer hovering three to four
tiles up, inside the jump's arc, passes. **Fix:** move the owl past the dig, and test flyers over their
whole rise.

**A4. minor — Bjørvika is the clumsy player's wall, not the finale.** At ±8 Bjørvika ends in Game over
63 times in 100 (Bygdøy 46, Vigelandsparken 25, Gamlebyen 13): the rebar at cols 44–47 and 64–67 (1.86
hearts a run) and the shafts with sliding sheets at 140–151 (1.63, 61 lives). From ±4 (no Game over) to
±8 is a cliff nowhere else in line 2. Not a fault for ±4 players; it means the penultimate level is the
one a struggling child gives up on.

**A5. nit (the tools) — three harness artifacts.** (a) The "mistakes" profile (`drop=0.03
overhold=0.15`) is unusable on line 2: a walk ends only within 2 px of its target at rest
(`game/bot.ts:58`), an over-held direction overshoots it, and the walk turns and overshoots again: 13 to
19 of 30 runs at Bjørvika, Vigelandsparken and Gamlebyen time out at "walk to …" with no heart lost. Line
1's review numbers for that profile carry the same inflation. (b) After a fall the route is resumed at the
move the perfect run was on at that place, which at Bygdøy's ferry is the walk onto the ferry, not the
wait for it: Bygdøy's 182 "hurt within 2 s of being put back" (all k together) are this. (c) A walk that overshoots onto
the sledge never ends (Korketrekkeren's 3 "off route" per 100). **Fix:** walks done within 4 px or after
a direction change; resume at the latest wait before the place.

**A-checked.** No Game over at ±2 anywhere; at ±4 at most 1 in 100 (sloppy) and 2 in 100 (hesitating);
no enemy hit came from an enemy on a 1920 × 1080 screen for under half a second; nobody was knocked into
water by a hit; walk-back after a fall is a median 0 to 3 s of the perfect run (Bjørvika's 90th
percentile 7.5 s, the shafts). Line 2 is fair for a careful or casual player; its problem is the curve (I).

### B. Softlocks and dead ends

**What I did.** (1) `mixed.ts`: Gamlebyen's rising stones in mixed states, searched with the tests' own
dead-end search (`search` + `deadEnds`, `src/game/reach.ts`) from the start, every flag and both benches:
every "all but one group" and "only one group" of its 11 groups (2 walls behind him, 3 ledge runs in St.
Clement's, 6 stepping stones), both walls risen with nothing else, and every state the perfect route
passes through (each time a stone starts to rise). A dead spot counts only where no unrisen ledge could
still rise; the walls behind him are never assumed, their states are enumerated. (2) `resume.ts` from all
49 flags and benches of line 2 at six clocks. (3) By reading and by simulation: the sledge, the statues,
the net and hooks, the lake.

**B1. major — there is no flag at the serpent's fight, so a lost life there costs the harbour again.** The
last flag is at col 216 (`levels/gamlebyen.ts:83`), before the basin where two bobbing longships are the
only way across (cols 222–235); the serpent's bench is at 243. Benches save only when he sits (`up` at
the bench). A player who walks past it and loses a life in the fight is put back at col 216.5
(`nobench.ts`: life lost at 30 s, put back at col 216.5, the serpent asleep again), and must make three
leaps onto moving decks over water to get back. Game over in the fight, by contrast, goes on from the bench
(`againAtBoss`, `game/play.ts:532`): losing all three lives is kinder than losing one. The troll and the
plough each have a flag beside their bench (DESIGN, "with a flag and a bench right before"). **Fix:** a
flag at col 244.

**B2. checked — Gamlebyen's stones in mixed states: no dead end.** 48 worlds: the 25 group states and
the 23 states the perfect route passes through (searched from 9 starts each, 4 700 to 18 000 spots per world, see `mixed.ts` output in "How to
rerun"): no spot from which neither the rune nor a fall can be reached that an unrisen ledge could not
rescue. The two walls behind him are a jump from the side he came (2 rows) and the flags at 96 and 182
stand past them, so no put-back lands behind a risen wall. Without the stone at 101 or at 121 the search
still crosses (the dash spans the gap); without any one of 106, 111, 116 it cannot, as intended.

**B3. checked — resumed from every flag and bench at six clocks, the perfect route reaches the rune**
from all 49 of line 2's. Three cost a heart at some clocks: Gamlebyen's flag 182 at all six (the dog at
col 210 meets him 6 s later, the same at every clock), Bygdøy's flag 31 (the gull at 58), Bjørvika's flag
93 (at two clocks the route falls off the first hook of the traverse at col 217: its hop is timed for the
beat the route was tuned to, `tools/review/tune-bjorvika.ts`).

**B4. checked — the sledge cannot stop, leave him or lose him.** Riding is decided by position alone
(`levels/korketrekkeren.ts:199`): between cols 33 and 250 his speed is set every step (never 0), the keys
he is left with are jump and duck, and every flag from col 70 on stands on the run, so a fall into the
gap or a lost life puts him on the run, on the sledge, and Game over starts the level again at the hut. Nothing on
the run is a wall. Holding down all the way ducks all the way and cannot jump the gap: 9 hearts in 90 s in
`shieldcases.ts`, as designed.

**B5. checked — the statues cannot trap him.** No solid tile within five rows over any statue's walk
(`statues.ts`), so a statue standing as a wall can always be jumped (16 px of 46). The one pit is the
courtyard (cols 138–153) between the pergola and the far wall, both three rows high; its only way out is
the statue, and the statue's walk (col 146 ± 7) covers the whole courtyard, so it can always be led to
either wall. Two statues facing each other (the rose garden's) hurt a player who dawdles between them
(that is the level), never hold him.

**B6. checked — Bjørvika's net and hooks, the lake.** The net under the first hook catches him and its
step (cols 75–76, row 19) leads back up; every hook comes right up to its far landing, so staying on means
riding back; a miss on the traverse falls to the dock and back to flag 213. A sunk lily pad is back 1.5 s
after he leaves it; falls in the long crossing put him on the stone (flag 181) or the island (138).

### C. The shield

**What I did.** Read `game/shield.ts` and its call in `game/play.ts:619`; `shieldcases.ts` for line 1's
bosses with the shield, the shield on ice, during a dash and on the sledge.

**C1. minor — a shield held at Nordmarka's arena entrance freezes the troll for ever, with no hint.**
Standing just inside the clearing with the shield up facing it, the troll walks into the shield, is
pushed back a tile (`game/troll.ts:238`), walks in again: 872 times in 300 s, no wind-up, no slam, no hint
(the "ten seconds of not getting nearer" rule never fires, since it does get nearer every time). In the
middle of the arena the club and the wave still come (Game over in 72 s, as without the shield). ADR 0024
says "the troll's club and wave go through, so Nordmarka's fight is unchanged" (`docs/adr/0024…:56`); it
is not, for a returning player who has the shield. **Fix:** let a shove count as "could not get nearer"
(back off to the far end with the hint), or let the troll ignore the shield.

**C2. minor (accepted in ADR 0024) — the plough cannot hurt a shield-holder.** Middle of the arena,
shield up: 1 heart in 300 s and 29 hints, against Game over in 80 s without it. Nothing is trivialised
(the hits still need the washing line, the dash and the tram), but on a replay the last boss of line 1 is
harmless.

**C3. minor — the help line never shows the shield or the duck.** The help line is hidden at his first
move (`packages/pixelkit/src/scene.ts:131`) and nothing in the viking page shows it again (only the maze
does, `main.ts:507`). `keysLine` (`main.ts:627–633`) faithfully rewrites its text when the shield is given
and while he rides the sledge, but by then it has been hidden since the first scene of the visit: a
player reaches the sledge 33 tiles into Korketrekkeren and the prow 198 tiles into Bygdøy, moving. Order
16's item 1 ("on the sledge the help line still says …") was fixed in text only, and a stepped screenshot
(nothing moved, so the line still shows) made it look done. **What does teach:** the gift's own bubble for
5 s ("The shield is yours. Hold down to raise it."), then the passage slinger 16 columns on, who hits a
careless walker every time; and the sign by the sledge (with a touch version). That is enough to learn by
being hit, but nothing says what the shield does (stops throws from in front, not from above). **Fix:**
show the help line for five seconds whenever its text changes and at each level's start.

**C4. minor (computed from `packages/pixelkit/src/stage.ts:48–51`, not seen) — the shield's first lesson
can come from a slinger off the screen.** Bygdøy's passage slinger sees 14 tiles (`levels/bygdoy.ts:198`,
224 px); `THROW_REACH` is 7 tiles so that "he always sees who throws" (`game/enemies.ts:66`). Facing it he
sees half the view plus 22 px ahead: 235 px at 1280 × 800 (fine), 214 px on a 1920 × 1080 screen, about
168 px on an upright phone at a device pixel ratio of 3 (240 at the least). There the first stone's wind-up
happens up to 56 px past the edge; the stone itself is seen for about 1.4 s, enough to raise the shield,
so it is fair, but the lesson is "something off screen hits you". Gamlebyen's archers and Bjørvika's
riveters (10 tiles) are just inside on a phone. **Fix:** reach 12 for the passage slinger, or a lookahead
that grows on a narrow view.

**C5. checked — every other way it could surprise.** At an edge: he stands. On a raft, a hook, the hoist:
carried as before. On a sinking pad: it sinks under him and he cannot jump until he lets go (a lily pad
holds 1.7 s). At a bench: up sits, down raises it; while seated down does nothing (`play.ts:619`). Turning
brings it round and raises it again in six steps. With the dash: down cannot start one, and a ground dash
already going ends before the shield is up (0 steps up while dashing). On ice: he slides about two tiles
with it up and stops. Click-to-walk never raises it (`careful.ts` gives no shield key); holding down during
a click-walk should stand him until he lets go (by reading, `main.ts:1107–1110`). On the sledge down is a duck and the shield stays packed (0 steps raised in a whole ride); if down is
still held as he leaves the sledge at Midtstuen, the shield starts to come up at once (by reading). Line 1's throwers, walkers and spiky things
are held off from the front as ADR 0024 says; the troll and plough are C1 and C2.

## 3. What I could not check, and why

- **Feel at real speed, sound, touch, gamepad.** As before, the Pi cannot run a browser at speed. Every
  timing is the rules stepped in Bun; ±k stands in for a person. The touch pad with five buttons, the
  Shield/Duck button and the sledge on a real phone were not seen.
- **The sloppy player is the route's plan with a person's timing.** It never finds a better line and never
  learns. Its shield is raised when the route raises it (just in time), so the shield stretches (A2) are
  pessimistic; its recovery after a fall has the artifacts in A5. The serpent strategies in D are scripts
  of what a person might do, not people.
- **The mixed-state search** covers 25 group states and the 23 states the perfect route passes through, not
  every subset of 28 stones (2^28); a state no route passes through and no group describes could in
  principle differ. Every stone that matters is a one-way ledge that only adds ground, and both walls were
  enumerated, so I do not expect one.
- **Line 1 at ±4** was rerun for the curve only (50 runs); many of its runs end off route (Akershus 35,
  Ekeberg 33 of 50) and its numbers are lower bounds. I did not look into why; that is line 1's.
- **Norwegian screenshots** were taken with a Firefox profile asking for nb-NO (`shots-lang.ts`); the help
  line could not be shown in Norwegian in a stepped shot after moving (C3).
- **The page after Gamlebyen's card** under `?debug&auto&steps=6200` showed the preview server's "not
  found" page; I did not find out why (it may be the autopilot acting on the card). Debug only, and not
  pursued.
