# Vikingfjorden: how it is built

A side-view game built the way the front page is built. The reasons (one cloth for all three games, the style bible) are in [`docs/games/PLAN.md`](../../../docs/games/PLAN.md), the ten levels with a picture of each in [`docs/games/LEVELS.html`](../../../docs/games/LEVELS.html); this file says what is here.

## The parts

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `src/levels/types.ts` | What a level is, as data: id, rune, name, landmark, pinned sky, the text map and its legend, ramps, carriers, ledges on a beat, cannons, zones (gusts on a beat among them), hazards, the dark, the troll, the last boss's rules, signs, doors, and its art. |
| `src/levels/langkaia.ts`, `akershus.ts`, `radhuset.ts`, `slottet.ts`, `holmenkollen.ts`, `akerselva.ts`, `nordmarka.ts`, `stortinget.ts`, `ekeberg.ts`, `nabolaget.ts` | The ten levels, each 220 to 280 columns, built with `build.ts` (strokes, not typed-out rows). |
| `src/levels/index.ts` | The ten in order (name, rune, place, built), and one loader per level: every level after Langkaia is fetched when first needed. |
| `src/levels/routes/` | The scripted routes through all ten levels, for the tests and for `?debug&auto`. Langkaia's route is its own small file, so the first script does not carry it. |
| `src/levels/kart.ts` | The line map, built as a level from the visitor's progress. |
| `src/game/play.ts` | The rules of a level, pure: a step function over the body, the clock, flags, cannons, drums, sheep, hearts and lives, pits, benches, skilling and the brown cheese, the dash, enemies, hazards, the troll and the last boss, the bracteate and the rune. The page and the tests run the same code. |
| `src/game/enemies.ts` | The five kinds of enemy as small pure rules: walker, spiky, flyer, thrower, jumper. |
| `src/game/plough.ts` | The snow plough in Nabolaget, the last boss, in the troll's shape: parked, waking, driving at him, revving for a second before every charge, stuck in the bank or turning; three hits on its cab (from the washing line, with the dash, from the tram's roof), and it parks. It comes with its level, through the level's boss slot (`LevelDef.boss`), which `play.ts` runs. |
| `src/game/troll.ts` | The troll of Nordmarka, the first boss, as a small pure rule: asleep, waking, walking, the club raised for a second before every slam, the wave, the dawn and the headlamp, three hits to stone. |
| `src/game/progress.ts` | `localStorage["viking.progress"]`: levels cleared, best time per level, bracteates found, most skilling per level, levels cleared without losing a heart, and a version mark (`v`). Forgiving of missing, old or broken data. A save without the mark (from before the first three levels were lengthened) keeps its runes and bracteates but loses the first three levels' best times, skilling and clean marks, once. |
| `src/main.ts` | The page: which scene the address shows, the header, the card, sounds and sparks, the camera, drawing, the maze. |
| `src/render/` | Tiles and water for any level, the far layers, the longship, sheep, runes, bracteates, the T-bane entrance. |

The kit (`packages/pixelkit`) holds the map reader, the physics with moving ground, surfaces (ice, an inrun, a belt) and zones that push (`zone.ts`, on a beat for a gust), hazards (`hazard.ts`), the dash, carriers on a path (stopping at stations), an orbit or a lift, flags, cannons and drums, ledges on a beat and pulses (`timed.ts`), the dark with lights through it (`dark.ts`, which the maze's dark level uses too), the camera, doors, props and the scene helpers. Swatches used by one level alone (`swatches/metro.ts`, `hill.ts`, `winter.ts`) are imported by that level directly, so they come in its file.

## The rules

- **Movement**: the kit's physics with the front page's constants, 60 steps a second: run 1.6, a jump 46 px high and 54 px far. The **raven's feather** from the maze gives a second jump in the air (91 px high, 101 px far): on when `localStorage["viking.feather"] === "1"`, or with `?feather`. It is read again when he comes out of the mound. With `viking.featherGold === "1"` the pompom and the feather pixels are gold.
- **A level** starts at `S` and ends at its rune: the clock stops, the rune flies to its slot in the header, and a card shows the time, the best, and the bracteate. "Onward" goes to the line map at the next station, "Once more" starts the level again.
- **Hearts and lives**: three hearts; an enemy's touch, something thrown, or a fall into water or a pit takes one, knocks him back and leaves him blinking and safe for a second and a half. Falls put him back at the last flag or bench (or the last firm ground where there is none yet). No hearts left: a life goes and he starts at the last flag or bench with full hearts. No lives left: a plain Game over card, and the level starts over with three lives; nothing cleared or found is lost. Lives and skilling go with him from level to level during a visit; 100 skilling or a slice of brown cheese is a life.
- **Benches**: up at a bench to sit; the hearts fill and the bench is where falls bring him back.
- **Enemies** (in every level): walkers and flyers are beaten by landing on them (higher with the jump held); spiky things hurt from above too; throwers lob on a beat after a one-second wind-up; jumpers leap out of water after a ripple. A beaten enemy puffs into squares and gives a skilling. Langkaia's pigeon on the quay is the first, on its own; Akershus's brazier is the first spiky thing, Rådhuset's chip-throwing gull the first thrower.
- **Surfaces**: ice has little grip, and the red rails stop a slide; an inrun has none at all, draws him on and lets his speed climb past a run; leaving the ground faster than a run keeps the speed in the air.
- **The ski pole** (from clearing Holmenkollen): a dash of three tiles in ten steps, level, once each time in the air. From Akerselva on he always has it, and some gaps need it.
- **Zones** (Akerselva's falls): a rectangle that pushes him while he is in it. A waterfall pushes down: under it a jump barely leaves the ground, and walking off an edge into one drops him in its pool. Jump over a fall's top, or dash through it: the dash ignores the push.
- **The dark** (Nordmarka): under the spruces only his headlamp's circle shows, a little round him and more the way he faces; lit windows, the blue trail marks, the sign boards, the bogs' water and the dawn show through, and eyes shine in it. The marsh and the clearings are lighter. The headlamp lights what stands in front of the backdrop (trees, walls, the ground's top edge); where its circle falls on the sky, the far layers or deeper into the soil, the night is put back.
- **The troll** (Nordmarka's end, with a flag and a bench right before): asleep it is a boulder; it wakes when he comes in, walks at him, raises its club for a full second, slams it (a wave runs along the ground: jump it), and the club sticks. Stuck where the dawn comes through the trees with his headlamp on it, a third of it turns to stone and it backs off; three times, and it is a boulder with rune N on it. A lost life sends it back to sleep, its stone kept.
- **Flags** (checkpoints): touched once, the front page's flag goes up the pole, and falls bring him back there. Forwards only.
- **Moving ground** (carriers): rafts that bob or drift (two on Langkaia drift together and stand side by side), a ferry that crosses, lifts, a hoist, a bell that swings, banners on a path, logs that drift and come round again, buckets on a water wheel and the tips of Rådhuset's clock hand (orbits), a moose's back. He is carried with them, and drawn on the same whole pixels as they are.
- **Cannons**: walk in, a third of a second in the barrel (the arc is shown, dotted), then the shot. In the air the keys only lean on the flight. A cannon he lands in takes him in: Akershus's saluting battery fires him from one to the next.
- **Ledges on a beat** (the bells): two seconds there, one second of the maze's warning shimmer, two gone. Neighbours are a step apart.
- **The gold bracteate**: one per level, only reachable with the feather. Kept once found, counted in the header.
- **Belts** (Stortinget): the escalators and a walkway carry him along, or against him, as he walks.
- **Trains** (Stortinget): carriers on a timetable that stand at the platforms; a board counts down to the next. Their roofs are the only way through the tunnels; a low beam sweeps a rider off at the next platform. In the cavern the express passes over the local, which goes down to the depot.
- **Hazards** (Stortinget): the third rail in the open track costs a heart, as an enemy's touch does, and throws him clear, back the way he came.
- **Gusts** (Ekeberg): zones on a beat. Leaves stream through for a second (the warning), then for a second and a half it pushes. Sheep may stand on their knolls (`sheepRange`); three of them bounce him over the quarry.
- **The plough** (Nabolaget's end, with a flag and a bench right before): see `plough.ts`. Its cab is too high to jump onto from the street.
- **The ending**: with all ten runes the helmet turns gold (`viking.crowned` is set), the header's runes are gold, the card says so once, in both languages, and the line map has all ten stations open. The Pi's light in the yellow house's window is red until Nabolaget's rune is taken, then green.
- **Doors**: T-banen at each level's start goes back to the map; Hjem goes to the front page (from Langkaia and the map to the Opera's door, from Nabolaget to the start of the front page); Runesmia (`/qr/`) and Verkstedet (GitHub) are sheds on Langkaia's quay, and Runesmia is also the smithy by the wheel at Hjula; Jernhaugen's mound in Nordmarka leads into the maze and back out to Nordmarka; the kiosk under Stortinget is Quintle's, padlocked with the front page's words until it is out. On the map, a station opens when the level before it is cleared.

## Checking

`bun test` plays each level with a scripted player (`src/game/bot.ts`) using the real rules: each must be cleared with the single jump, raising every flag, without losing a heart, and taking at least a minute (Nabolaget ninety seconds; 6 to 10 with the ski pole); with the feather each bracteate is reached. Every level is held to the order's measure: 220 to 280 columns, 40 to 80 skilling, eight to twenty enemies, one brown cheese, one or two benches, two to four signs, no enemy at a flag or a bench. A search with the real `stepBody` (`src/game/reach.ts`), over a world where every moving thing is everywhere at once, every ledge on a beat is always there and every enemy and sheep is a trampoline all along its patrol, with the ski pole's dash, proves each bracteate cannot be reached without the feather; zones push as they do in the level, gusts searched blowing and calm; standing on moving ground he may drop through it, as he does when it moves away; the ten searches run side by side in workers. `rules.test.ts` tests hearts, lives, pits, benches, skilling, the enemies, the drum, the dash, zones and the troll on small made-up levels, and hazards, gusts, sheep on a knoll and the plough. Screenshots come from the harness (`?debug&steps=N&keys=...`, `?debug&auto`, `tools/shots.ts`).
