# Vikingfjorden

The game behind the secret door at the top of the Opera on [larshansen.dev](https://larshansen.dev), served at `/viking/`. The same person, with a viking helmet over his hat, takes the T-bane through Oslo: a line map with a stop for each rune of LARS HANSEN, and a level at each stop. All ten are built: Langkaia (L), Akershus festning (A), Rådhuset (R), Slottet (S: 17 May up Karl Johan), Holmenkollen (H: ice and the ski jump), Akerselva (A: down the river, its falls and the wheel at Hjula), Nordmarka (N: the forest at night, by headlamp, and the troll at dawn), Stortinget (S: the escalators, the third rail, the trains' roofs through the tunnels), Ekeberg (E: the tram up the hill, gusts of wind, three sheep over the quarry, and the city lit below) and Nabolaget (N: home over the roofs of Vålerenga, past the snow plough, to the yellow house). A branch of the line dips to Jernhaugen, the burial mound with the maze in it (`packages/maze`). He has three hearts and some lives; pigeons, gulls, rats, snowball kids, salmon, foxes, magpies and cats are in the way; benches heal; skilling make lives; clearing Holmenkollen gives the ski pole, a dash. With all ten runes the helmet turns gold.

It is built exactly like the front page (`apps/landing`): plain TypeScript, Canvas 2D, pixel art drawn in code with rectangles, and the shared kit in `packages/pixelkit`. There is no image, font or audio file. The why is in [`docs/games/PLAN.md`](../../docs/games/PLAN.md), the level plan in [`docs/games/LEVELS.html`](../../docs/games/LEVELS.html).

```bash
bun run dev:viking     # from the repo root: http://localhost:5173/viking/
bun run build:viking   # type-check + production build
bun test               # in apps/viking: maps, scripted players, reach, progress
```

- How it works, what it contains and how to check it: [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md).
- Addresses: `#kart` (the line map), `#langkaia`, `#akershus`, `#radhuset`, `#slottet`, `#holmenkollen`, `#akerselva`, `#nordmarka`, `#stortinget`, `#ekeberg`, `#nabolaget`, `#jernhaugen` (the maze). The mound's door in Nordmarka also leads into the maze, and out of it he comes back to Nordmarka as he left it. Back and Forward move between them. Every level after Langkaia is its own small script, fetched the first time it is needed.
- Useful URL parameters: `?debug` (exposes `window.__game`, and shows any error on the help line), `?debug&level=<id>` (any level), `?x=<px>&y=<px>` (places him), `?sky=day|evening|twilight|night|winter|17mai`, `?feather` (the double jump), `?pole` (the dash), `?debug&steps=N&keys=...` (the harness, see PLAN.md section 9; `d` in `keys` is the dash), `?debug&auto&steps=N` (levels 4 to 10 play their tests' scripted route, for looking at), `?debug&hearts=1&lives=1` (start with fewer), `?debug&cleared=langkaia,akershus&gold=langkaia&clean=akershus` (pretend progress, not stored). Keys: arrows or WASD, Space to jump, up at a door or a bench, Shift or X to dash (with the pole), R starts the level again, M mutes, T steps through the skies.
