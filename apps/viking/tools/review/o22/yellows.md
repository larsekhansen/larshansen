# Order 22, A4: every yellow and gold, and what became of it

The rule to end with: **flat saturated yellow on a top edge means "stand here", and nothing else in the game is that
yellow in that shape.** The paint is `STAND.top`, `#ffd400`. The levels' own colours were listed with
`bun tools/glance/yellows.ts` (each level drawn as the palette test draws it, with what moves and what is in front,
by day; hue 35 to 70 degrees, saturation 0.3 or more; "share" is of everything the level paints; distances are
CIEDE2000 from the paint and from the gold of what he takes, `COIN.gold`, `#c8902a`). What the page and the kit draw
outside the levels is listed by hand. The palette test now holds that no colour any level paints is within 10 of the
paint but the paint itself, its worn ends and the two named below.
