Order 28 asked three questions of every flat thing (ADR 0051); the same columns are used here. "Flat" means drawn as plain
rectangles, one colour a part, with no lit side, no shadowed foot and no grain, or (for a carrier) drawn so that it reads as
something else. Most of what order 28 left alone still stands up at four screen pixels an art pixel; three things let their
surroundings down clearly, and they are the ones redrawn.
