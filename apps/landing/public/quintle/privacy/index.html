Privacy Policy — Quintle

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Quintle collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

Quintle (“the app”) is a word-puzzle game published by Lars Hansen.

What we collect

Nothing. Quintle does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no account or login.
  • The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your game progress, statistics, and settings are stored only on your device using Android’s local storage, and never leave it.
  • The app requires no runtime permissions. It uses the device vibrator only for optional in-app haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Quintle is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
